# SignalStack AI — 30-day publishing system

## Objective
Build a global English-language audience around practical AI-tool demonstrations and route qualified viewers to relevant affiliate offers.

## Cadence
- 2 short videos/day = 60 pieces in 30 days.
- 1 weekly 4–8 minute compilation/tutorial = 4 long-form videos.
- Republish the same original vertical master to YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels and TikTok.
- Never upload unedited third-party clips.

## Daily format
1. 0–2s: concrete problem.
2. 2–12s: live screen demonstration.
3. 12–22s: result.
4. 22–28s: limitation or caveat.
5. Final seconds: one clear CTA.

## Content pillars
- "I tested X so you don't have to."
- "3 clicks to do X."
- "Before/after workflow."
- "Hidden feature."
- "Tool A vs Tool B" — describe differences, do not manufacture claims.
- "Free alternative."
- "What this AI cannot do."

## Monetization order
1. Affiliate links on tools actually demonstrated.
2. YouTube fan-funding/Shopping eligibility as the audience grows.
3. Full YouTube ad monetization when eligible.
4. Original digital product after repeated audience demand is visible.

## Metrics
Track per post:
- Views
- Average watch percentage
- Completion rate
- Saves/shares
- Profile clicks
- Link clicks
- Affiliate signups
- Paid conversions
- Revenue per 1,000 views

## Kill rules
After 20 posts in a topic, keep topics that generate above-median retention and clicks. Rework or stop topics that repeatedly produce low retention and no clicks.

## Rules
- English-first and global.
- No personal name in branding.
- No cold-DM sales.
- No fake testimonials.
- No fake earnings claims.
- No bots or artificial views.
- Disclose affiliate relationships.
- Use original demonstrations, narration and editing.
