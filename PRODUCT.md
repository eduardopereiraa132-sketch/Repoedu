# Vendor Intelligence Agent

## Positioning

AI-powered vendor due-diligence workflow for procurement, security and compliance teams.

**Promise:** turn a vendor URL and supporting documents into a structured, evidence-oriented vendor review in minutes, then automate the follow-up work.

## Product workflow

1. User submits vendor URL.
2. Public website signals are collected.
3. Vendor documents are uploaded as text/PDF-derived text.
4. The system extracts obligations, dates, amounts, security signals and missing evidence.
5. A vendor assessment is assembled with explicit limitations and evidence.
6. Missing information is converted into a supplier-question checklist.
7. Future phase: generate and track supplier follow-up emails and re-assess responses.

## What it is not

- Not a penetration test.
- Not a certification.
- Not legal advice.
- Not a replacement for human approval of material procurement/security decisions.

## Revenue strategy

The existing x402 endpoints remain the machine-facing primitives. The commercial product sells the workflow and business outcome rather than individual API calls.

Primary metric: annual recurring revenue per customer.

Secondary metric: vendor assessments completed per customer and percentage requiring human intervention.
