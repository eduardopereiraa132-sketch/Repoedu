The fastest commercial test should be B2B partners that already sell IT/cybersecurity services and can resell a vendor-review workflow to multiple clients.

## Why partner-led distribution

White-label/partner distribution is already a recognized model in cybersecurity: Securicom explicitly markets white-label TPRM and vCISO services to MSP/MSSP partners, while Black Kite markets an MSSP partner program for third-party risk. This validates the channel concept, not our product-market fit.

## Outreach objective

Do not sell a generic AI product. Ask whether the partner has customers where vendor reviews, supplier questionnaires, security evidence collection or third-party risk assessments consume analyst time.

Offer a 10-vendor pilot and let the partner put its own service wrapper around the workflow.

## Qualification trigger

A target becomes high-fit when it:

- already sells cybersecurity/compliance/managed services;
- has recurring B2B customers;
- performs vendor or third-party assessments manually;
- can resell or white-label services;
- has a customer base large enough that 10–50 automated assessments/month are plausible.

Sources: Clutch Uruguay MSP/cybersecurity directories; Securicom MSSP white-label page; Black Kite MSSP partner page.
