# Pilot sales message

## Email / LinkedIn

Subject: Vendor reviews taking too long?

Hi — I’m building a vendor due-diligence agent that automates the repetitive part of supplier reviews: public-site checks, document extraction, obligations, security signals, missing evidence and the follow-up questions needed to close gaps.

It is designed as a first-pass layer for procurement/security teams, with human approval remaining on material decisions.

I’m opening a small number of pilot deployments. The pilot is designed around a real set of vendors rather than a generic demo.

If vendor reviews are currently spread across PDFs, email and spreadsheets, I can show you the workflow on one of your public vendors.

## Qualification questions

1. Approximately how many new or renewed vendors do you review each month?
2. How long does one review normally take?
3. Which documents/questionnaires are involved?
4. Where are the final assessments stored?
5. Which steps require repeated supplier follow-up?

## Pilot offer

US$2,500 setup + US$500/month for the initial pilot.

Success criteria: measurable reduction in analyst time, fewer manual extraction steps, and a clear audit trail of what was observed versus what still requires human review.
