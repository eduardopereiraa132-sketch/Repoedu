AI-assisted vendor due diligence for procurement, security and compliance teams.

## Why companies buy it

Vendor reviews are often spread across email, PDFs, spreadsheets and browser tabs. The result is slow onboarding, repeated questionnaires and missed renewal or security obligations.

Vendor Intelligence Agent turns that work into one repeatable workflow.

## What the assessment produces

- Public website security preflight
- Vendor security signals
- Contract/document obligations
- Dates and monetary commitments
- Missing evidence and control gaps
- Supplier questions to resolve gaps
- Executive summary
- Explicit limitations and human-review points

## Trust model

The product distinguishes observed evidence from inference and clearly states what it cannot verify. It is a first-pass due-diligence system, not a penetration test, certification or legal opinion.

## FAQ

### Does it replace security or procurement staff?
No. It automates collection, extraction, triage and follow-up preparation. Material decisions remain with authorized people.

### Does it test a vendor's systems?
No. The initial product uses public website signals and supplied documents. It does not perform penetration testing.

### Can it work with our own questionnaire?
Yes as a product direction. Custom requirements are part of the paid pilot/enterprise scope.
