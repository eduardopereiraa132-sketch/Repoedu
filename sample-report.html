01 / EXECUTIVE SNAPSHOT

What was observable?

  • HTTPS and transport configuration were checked as public-site signals.

02 / EVIDENCE GAPS

What still needs proof?

  • Current independent assurance evidence, where applicable.
  • Incident response and notification commitments.
  • Privacy, retention/deletion and subprocessor details.
  • Business continuity and recovery testing evidence.
03 / FOLLOW-UP QUESTIONS

What should the supplier answer?

  • What current independent security assurance is available?
  • What incident notification commitment applies?
  • What data retention and deletion obligations apply?
  • Which subprocessors are currently authorized?
