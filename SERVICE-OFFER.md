# Productized Service — PDF / Excel Data Cleanup & Reporting

## Offer

Turn messy PDFs, Excel exports, CSVs and business reports into a clean, analysis-ready Excel/Google Sheets file.

The service is deliberately outcome-based: the buyer receives a usable dataset, not raw copy/paste work.

## Why this offer

Current marketplace research shows strong 2026 demand growth for Excel data cleaning and PDF-to-Excel work. Fiverr reported +210% search growth for Excel data cleaning and +153% for PDF-to-Excel conversion; Upwork also reports that AI-enabled skills are growing rapidly while human judgment and execution remain in demand. These are search/demand signals, not guaranteed sales.

## What the client sends

1. The PDF/Excel/CSV files.
2. Desired output format (Excel, CSV or Google Sheets).
3. Any business-specific rules, e.g. date format, currency, duplicate definition.
4. Optional sample of the expected final layout.

No passwords, seed phrases or payment credentials are requested.

## Delivery QA

Every delivery should be checked for:

- row/record counts
- duplicate records
- missing values
- date consistency
- numeric/currency parsing
- totals against the source when totals are available
- column names and data types
- obvious extraction errors
- output file opening correctly

## Scope limits

This service does not promise legal, accounting, tax or financial advice. It transforms and validates data supplied by the client. Ambiguous business rules must be confirmed by the client.

## Fast-sale strategy

Prioritize buyers who already have a concrete file and a deadline. The fastest path is a small fixed-price first order, followed by a recurring monthly workflow when the same report repeats.
