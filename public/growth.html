Trust boundary

Evidence, not invented certainty.

The service reports public signals and supplied evidence. It does not claim certification, penetration testing, legal advice or proof that a vendor is secure.

All commercial pricing beyond the published API and fixed-scope review is negotiated around scope and volume.
