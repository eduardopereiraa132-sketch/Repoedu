Why teams buy this

The repetitive part of vendor review is expensive even when the final decision stays human.

EvidenceCheck is built for the work before the substantive assessment: collecting public signals, reviewing supplied text, identifying missing evidence and preparing supplier questions.

Collect

Make visible evidence reusable

Website posture, headers, cookie flags, security.txt, page content, metadata, dates and obligations arrive in consistent structures.

Separate

Unknown is not the same as absent

Missing public evidence is reported as a gap—not silently converted into a claim that a control does not exist.

Act

Turn gaps into supplier questions

The reviewer gets a concrete queue of what to request, verify or escalate next.

Trust boundary

Useful without pretending to be more than it is.

EvidenceCheck is a first-pass due-diligence workflow. It is not a penetration test, vulnerability scanner, certification, legal opinion or automated approval engine. Public signals are signals, not proof. Material findings should be verified by the responsible team.
