The buying problem

The expensive part often starts before the review.

Before a security or procurement decision, teams repeatedly collect public evidence, inspect supplier material, write notes and turn missing information into follow-up questions. EvidenceCheck is built for that repetitive layer.

Trust boundary

EvidenceCheck is a first-pass due-diligence workflow. It is not a penetration test, vulnerability scanner, certification, legal opinion or automated approval engine. Public signals are signals, not proof. Material findings should be verified by the responsible team.
