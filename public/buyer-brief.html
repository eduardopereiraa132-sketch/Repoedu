Trust boundary

Useful evidence without pretending it is proof.

EvidenceCheck does not claim certification, legal conclusions, penetration testing or complete security assurance. Public signals are signals. Material findings should be verified by the responsible team.

Does this replace a security review?

No. It is designed to make the first pass faster and more structured, then hand the decision back to the authorized reviewer.

Can an AI agent buy it directly?

Yes. Paid API routes expose x402 payment terms, OpenAPI and agent-discovery metadata for compatible automated clients.

Do I need a subscription?
