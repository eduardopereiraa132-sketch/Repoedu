Trust boundary

Useful because it knows what it cannot prove.

Does this certify a vendor?

No. It reports observable evidence and gaps. Public signals are signals, not proof of certification, compliance or security.

Is this a penetration test?

No. It is a bounded first-pass due-diligence workflow, not an authorized penetration test or vulnerability scanner.

Can an AI agent buy it directly?

Yes. The service publishes machine-readable discovery, OpenAPI and x402 payment metadata for compatible agent workflows.

What if evidence is missing?

Missing public evidence is reported as a gap. The service does not infer that a control does not exist simply because it was not found publicly.
