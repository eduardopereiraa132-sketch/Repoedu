The problem

The first pass is repetitive — and it creates the evidence trail for everything after it.

Teams repeatedly open supplier websites, collect basic security signals, search for documents, note what is missing and write follow-up questions. EvidenceCheck makes that collection step repeatable without pretending that public signals are proof of compliance or security.

Observe

Collect the visible signals

Website posture, security headers, cookie flags, security.txt, robots.txt, page content and other observable signals.

Separate

Make the unknowns explicit

“Not observed publicly” is kept distinct from “does not exist.” Missing evidence becomes a concrete gap to verify.

Act

Generate the next questions

Turn gaps into supplier follow-ups instead of rebuilding a checklist from scratch for every vendor.

Trust boundary

Useful without overclaiming.

Does EvidenceCheck certify a vendor?

No. It reports observable public signals, supplied information and explicit gaps. It is not a certification, penetration test, vulnerability assessment, legal opinion or substitute for authorized human review.

Why is the API inexpensive?

The machine endpoints are deliberately narrow primitives. They are designed to be composed into larger workflows rather than sold as a large all-in-one platform.

Can an AI agent buy the service?

Yes. The service publishes machine-readable discovery material and supports per-request USDC payment on Base through x402.
