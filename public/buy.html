Trust boundary

Useful because it is explicit about what it cannot prove.

Does EvidenceCheck certify a vendor?

No. It reports observable evidence and gaps. Public signals are signals, not proof of certification, compliance or security.

Is this a penetration test?

No. It is a bounded first-pass due-diligence workflow using public website signals and supplied business evidence. It does not perform authenticated testing or exploitation.

Does it replace procurement or security staff?

No. It automates collection, extraction, triage and follow-up preparation so authorized reviewers can focus on decisions that require judgment.

Can an AI agent buy a single capability?

Yes, where the client supports x402. Machine-readable discovery and pricing metadata expose the paid routes so compatible agents can discover and pay per request.

What should a buyer do with a “missing” control?

Treat it as a verification request, not proof of absence. Ask the supplier for the evidence your policy requires and route material decisions through the responsible reviewer.
