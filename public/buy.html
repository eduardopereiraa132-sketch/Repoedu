Trust boundary

Useful first-pass evidence — not a fake certification.

EvidenceCheck does not claim that public signals prove security, compliance, legal validity or absence of vulnerabilities. It is not a penetration test, certification, legal opinion or replacement for an authorized human decision.

Questions
Who is this for?

Procurement, third-party risk, security and compliance teams that need a repeatable first-pass vendor review, plus AI agents that need structured evidence primitives.

Why pay per request?

It lets an automated workflow call only the checks it needs instead of buying a large subscription before proving value.

What does the $0.025 vendor preflight prove?

It provides a structured first-pass based on available evidence. It does not prove that a vendor is secure or compliant.
