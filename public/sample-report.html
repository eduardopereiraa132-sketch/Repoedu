What the buyer actually receives

A structured starting point — not a black-box verdict.

This illustrative report shows the shape of a vendor preflight: observable signals, evidence gaps, supplier questions and explicit limitations. It uses fictional findings so no real company is misrepresented.

01 · Observable

Signals we can actually see

  • HTTPS: public site is served over HTTPS.
  • Security headers: selected browser-facing headers can be inspected.
  • Security contact: security.txt presence can be checked.
  • Public content: policy and security-related pages can be extracted for review.
02 · Evidence gaps

What public signals do not prove

  • Whether access controls are implemented correctly internally.
  • Whether backups are tested and restorable.
  • Whether incident response exercises occur.
  • Whether claimed certifications are current and in scope.
03 · Supplier questions

What to ask next

  • Can you provide the current security certification or assurance report and scope?
  • How are privileged accounts reviewed and removed?
  • When was the last restore test for critical backups?
  • What evidence can you provide for incident-response testing?
