FAQ
Is this a certification or security assessment?

No. It is a first-pass evidence workflow. Material findings require appropriate human verification and authorized deeper review where needed.

Can this be automated?

Yes. The machine API exposes pay-per-request endpoints using x402 and USDC on Base. The pilot is the human-led entry point.

What if the public evidence is incomplete?

That is part of the output: missing evidence is surfaced explicitly and translated into questions or verification actions.
