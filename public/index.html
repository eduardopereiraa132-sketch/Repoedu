FAQ

Before you buy

Is this a penetration test?

No. It is a first-pass public-signal and evidence workflow. It does not replace authorized testing, certification review or a human security decision.

Do I need a subscription to call the API?

No subscription is required for the machine endpoints. Compatible clients pay per request through x402.

Why use it instead of a generic AI model?

The workflow is deliberately bounded around observable evidence, explicit gaps, supplier questions and machine-readable outputs rather than an unconstrained summary.

Can an AI agent buy it?

Yes. The service exposes machine-readable discovery metadata and x402 payment requirements intended for compatible agents.
