The buyer problem

Most vendor review does not begin with a hard security assessment. It begins with repetitive evidence work.

Someone still has to collect public signals, read supplied material, identify missing evidence, prepare follow-up questions and package the result for a reviewer. EvidenceCheck is designed for that layer.

Collect

Make evidence reusable

Website posture, headers, cookie flags, security.txt, page content, metadata, dates and obligations arrive in consistent structures.

Separate

Unknown ≠ absent

Act

Reviewers get a concrete queue of what to request, verify or escalate next.

FAQ

Before you buy

Does a missing public signal mean the vendor fails?

No. EvidenceCheck treats missing public evidence as a gap to verify, not as proof that the underlying control is absent.

Can an AI agent use it without a dashboard?

Yes. The project exposes OpenAPI and agent-discovery resources plus pay-per-request endpoints designed for machine workflows.

What happens after the first pass?

The output becomes a verification queue: request evidence, confirm with the supplier, escalate selected items and complete the organization's substantive review.

Is the API the same product as the human review?

They share the evidence-first workflow but serve different buyers. The API is composable infrastructure; the human-facing offer is a defined review service.
