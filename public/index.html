Questions buyers usually ask

Clear boundaries build trust.

Does this prove a vendor is secure?

No. It reports observable public signals and explicitly identifies missing evidence. Material conclusions require appropriate evidence and human judgment.

Why pay per request?

Many workflows need one narrow capability rather than a new platform. The low entry price makes the capability easy to trial and compose.

Can an AI agent use it?

Yes. The service publishes OpenAPI and machine-readable discovery metadata and supports x402 payment in USDC on Base for machine endpoints.

Can teams use it for recurring vendor triage?

Yes. The API can be composed into recurring workflows; higher-volume use and additional human review can be scoped commercially.
