Built with a boundary

Useful evidence is not the same thing as proof.

EvidenceCheck reports observable public signals. It is not a penetration test, certification, vulnerability scanner or legal opinion. Material findings should be validated by the responsible human or security team.

Questions

What buyers should know.

Does EvidenceCheck replace a security assessment?

No. It is a first-pass evidence and signal workflow. Its job is to reduce repetitive triage and make gaps explicit before deeper review.

What data does it use?

The public-facing website capabilities are designed around publicly accessible web evidence. They do not turn public signals into claims of certification or compliance.

Why pay per request?

Agent workflows often need one small capability at a time. Pay-per-request lets software call only what it needs instead of buying a large seat-based product.

How does payment work?

The paid API uses x402-style request payments in USDC on Base. The buyer receives a payment requirement and the result is returned after settlement.
