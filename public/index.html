Boundaries

Useful because it knows what it cannot prove.

01

Evidence ≠ assurance

Public evidence can inform a review; it does not certify a vendor or establish security by itself.

02

Unknown stays unknown

Absence of public evidence is reported as a gap, not as proof that a control does not exist.

03

Human decision stays human

Material findings should be verified by the responsible procurement, security or risk team.

FAQ

Simple enough to evaluate quickly.

What is EvidenceCheck?

A first-pass vendor due-diligence workflow that converts public signals and supplied business text into structured evidence, gaps, supplier questions and next actions.

Is it a vulnerability scanner or penetration test?

No. It is explicitly not a penetration test, vulnerability scanner, certification, legal opinion or replacement for an authorized assessment.

Can an AI agent buy it directly?

Yes. The machine endpoints are designed for x402 pay-per-request access using USDC on Base, with discovery metadata and structured JSON responses.

Do you claim guaranteed security or approval?

No. The product is decision support. It reports observable evidence and gaps and leaves the accountable decision to the buyer.
