FAQ

What EvidenceCheck is — and isn't.

Does a public signal prove a vendor is secure?

No. The service reports observable signals and separates them from evidence that requires documents, authenticated access, testing or independent validation.

Who is the service for?

Procurement, third-party risk, security/compliance teams and software agents that need a repeatable vendor first-pass.

Why pay per request?

Because many workflows need a narrow capability only occasionally. Per-request pricing makes it possible to test and compose the service without a recurring subscription.

How does the API payment work?

Paid machine endpoints use x402 with USDC on Base. Compatible clients can discover the price, settle the request and receive the structured response.
