What it is not

First-pass evidence, not a certification.

EvidenceCheck does not certify a vendor, perform a penetration test, provide a legal opinion or prove that a supplier is secure or compliant. Material findings require appropriate human verification.

That boundary is intentional: the product is designed to make the next human decision faster and better informed.

Questions buyers ask

Clear boundaries before payment.

What exactly am I paying for?

A narrowly defined machine task. For example, a website audit returns observable public security signals; a vendor preflight turns those signals into evidence gaps and follow-up questions.

Does this replace a security or compliance review?

No. It is a first-pass evidence tool. It helps decide what deserves verification; it does not certify a vendor or replace professional review.

Why pay per request?

Because many workflows need occasional checks rather than another large subscription. Micropayments make a single useful request economically viable for both human and agent buyers.

Can an AI agent buy it?

Yes. The service publishes machine-readable documentation and supports x402-style payment flows on Base for paid endpoints.
