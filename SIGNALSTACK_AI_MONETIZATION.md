# SIGNALSTACK AI — MONETIZATION STACK

## 1. Primary: affiliate revenue
Start with tools that are directly demonstrated in the videos.

### OpusClip
Official affiliate program: https://www.opus.pro/affiliate
Current published terms: 25% recurring commission during the first year for referred subscribers; approval is required; payouts are in USD through PayPal; first payout requires at least $20 in commission. The program says there is no minimum sales requirement to apply.

### ElevenLabs
Official affiliate program: https://elevenlabs.io/affiliates
Current published terms: up to 22% on qualifying Starter/Creator/Pro/Scale subscriptions for the first 12 months; Business is 11%; payments use PartnerStack and are subject to the program's payment/eligibility rules.

## 2. Secondary: YouTube monetization
Do NOT build the business around Shorts ad revenue initially. Current YPP ad-revenue eligibility requires 1,000 subscribers plus either 4,000 valid public watch hours in 12 months or 10 million valid Shorts views in 90 days. YouTube also has an expanded YPP tier at 500 subscribers plus activity/view thresholds in eligible countries.

## 3. Later: original digital product
After audience validation, create a small original product around the audience's proven problem, such as a tested AI workflow/template pack. Do not create it before the channel shows which problem people actually care about.

## 4. Content-to-money funnel
Short → profile/link page → relevant tool → affiliate commission.

## 5. What we are NOT doing
- no cold-DM selling
- no scraped email lists
- no fake engagement
- no reposting copyrighted clips
- no fake income claims
- no impersonation of software brands
- no paid ads using affiliate links where a program prohibits them
