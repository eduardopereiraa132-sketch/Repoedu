FAQ

What the service is — and is not.

Is this a penetration test?

No. It is a first-pass evidence and public-signal review. It does not perform authenticated testing, exploitation or vulnerability scanning.

Does “unknown” mean the vendor failed?

No. Unknown means the evidence was not verified by this first pass. The right next step is to request evidence or perform the appropriate human review.

Why a fixed price?

The first offer is deliberately narrow: one vendor, one defined scope, one review artifact. That makes the purchase easy to understand before discussing recurring work.

Can agents buy it?

Yes. The API exposes machine-readable capabilities and x402 payment terms for compatible software that wants to pay per request.

What is the delivery promise?

The commercial offer uses a target 48-hour turnaround after the required information is received. It is a target, not a guarantee.

What happens to the evidence?

The product records the assessment result so the buyer can retrieve or render the review artifact. Sensitive material should only be supplied when the buyer's workflow permits it.
