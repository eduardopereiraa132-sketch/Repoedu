## Publishing rules
- Publish original footage and narration.
- Use English for the global audience.
- Keep videos 15–35 seconds initially.
- Put the proof/demo before the explanation.
- Never fake views, likes, comments or testimonials.
- Never claim guaranteed earnings.
- For affiliate videos, disclose the relationship clearly.
- Track retention, rewatches, profile clicks and affiliate conversions.
